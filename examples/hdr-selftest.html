HDR canvas self-test

No scene, no gain maps: every patch value below is written straight into the swapchain, so this page only measures the canvas and the display.

Test 1 — which number does the canvas want?

Three grey surrounds (CSS #404040 #808080 #bfbfbf) wrap a pair of canvas patches each. A holds the sRGB-encoded number, B holds the same colour as a linear number. Exactly one of them should vanish into its surround — squint, or look from a step back.
A vanishes → the swapchain is extended sRGB (gamma-encoded): shaders must apply the sRGB OETF.
B vanishes → the swapchain is linear (scRGB-style): shaders must write linear light and not encode.

Test 2 — is the display actually HDR?

Patches 1.25 … 4.0 are written raw. Under the encoded reading they are 1.7× … 25× SDR white, under the linear reading 1.25× … 4×; either way they must be visibly brighter than the 1.0 patch, and they stop separating once you pass the display headroom. The strip at the bottom of the canvas sweeps 0 → 4: where it stops brightening is the headroom you really have.
